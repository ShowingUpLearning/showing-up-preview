How Curious Are You?

Discover your natural curiosity style.

Curiosity is one of the greatest gifts we have: the instinct to look just beyond the obvious and find something new. But we don’t all express our curiosity and explore the world in the same way. Some of us seek knowledge and understanding of how things work. Some of us thrive on novelty, energy, and ideas. And some lead with people, emotion, and connection.

This short reflection helps you notice which form of curiosity you naturally gravitate towards, at work and in the rest of your life. There are no right answers, only accurate ones. So answer as you actually are, rather than as you’d like to be. Most people lean heavily towards one style, and knowing which one is where the useful work starts.

Primary style

 

 

Whichever letter you have most of is your natural preference: mostly A’s is epistemic, mostly B’s is diversive, mostly C’s is empathetic. If you have a spike in one form, that is a great strength. You just need to be aware that you sit lower on the other two, and that could be a gap in how you approach your work and your ideas. Every team needs to ensure that between you, you have a balance, and always seek to develop the forms that are less of a natural preference for you.

What to do next

Think of the three forms as a tripod. If one leg is short, the whole thing wobbles and you can’t get a clear picture. Your natural spike is your edge, so never diminish it.
