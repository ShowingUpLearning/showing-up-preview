The Showing Up Learning Platform

The Nine Dimensions™ are what your team develops. The six parts of the platform are how.

Use the platform to strengthen the training you already run, or make it your team's entire development programme: it works both ways. Everything is available the moment it's needed, so the learning fits around client work. You'll notice the difference the very next time your team is in front of a client.

1Define

Defining what great looks like

What's asked of a consultant changes with every step: the first client role, leading a business unit, making partner. Define gives your team one Career Development Map for the whole journey, with five clear levels for all Nine Dimensions™. Each person can see exactly where they are, and the level they're reaching for next.

Explore the career map Download the Curiosity map
2Assess

See where you shine, and where you'll grow

Once great is defined, the next question is where each person stands. Assess provides in-depth 360 assessments, which can be completed alone or with a manager and peers adding their view. Every consultant comes away knowing their greatest strengths, what to build on and what to work on. And every manager comes away knowing how to best support.

Explore the assessments Try the curiosity self-assessment
3Learn

Bite-size, engaging video learning

Video lessons are the centrepiece of the platform: short, sharp, and built to be used the same day. Every module gives your team something to carry straight into their next piece of client work, watched alone or together as a team. You can experience one lesson here; just press play. It runs three minutes.

4Toolkits

Downloadable toolkits

80% of learning is forgotten within 30 days, and that's the problem Toolkits exists to solve. Every module comes with a downloadable toolkit of key models, exercises and reflections, so each lesson becomes something your team puts into practice, not something they once watched. 5Play

Team games & challenges

The games are quick, competitive, and made to be played together: a manager can lead, or the team can simply hit play and go. Every game relates to the real world of consulting, so while it feels like play, your team is sharpening how they think, how they work together, and how they show up for clients. 6Lead

Investing in your people-managers

Managers play a crucial role in development. Lead trains your managers to be brilliant at helping their team reach their greatest potential. It provides video modules and toolkits on coaching well, giving feedback that lands, guiding development, and running the platform for their team. The aim is simple: managers who coach, not just manage.
