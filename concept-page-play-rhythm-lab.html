Three ways to give the Play page a rhythm

Ben, on the Play page as it went live this afternoon:

"Section background colours, to break up the page rhythm so no two consecutive sections share a colour. Two ways to play → white, not cream. Manager-led sessions → black. Immediate impact, lasting unity → coral."

The test is right: three bands running together read as one scroll. All three options below pass it. They differ in what they spend to get there, and option A spends the one thing the page uses for emphasis.

The one thing worth seeing full size

Both headings below are the real thing at the real size, with the coral accent exactly as the page carries it today.

Immediate impact, lasting unity
On cream. The accent does its job: one word carries the emphasis, the way every other heading on the site works.
Immediate impact, lasting unity
On coral. "Unity" is still marked up as the accent. It is the same colour as the ground behind it, so the emphasis is gone, and any coral pill or chip placed in this band would go the same way.

This is not an argument against coral. It is an argument about where coral goes. As a background it removes emphasis from everything inside it; as a fill on the four boxes it creates the strongest moment on the page and keeps the accent system working. Option B spends it on the cards for that reason.

Two smaller notes. Pure white is not a canvas colour in the palette — cream is the default and white is for reversed text and button labels — and next to cream it is the smallest step available, so it separates least where Ben wants most. And whatever we pick should be a rule for all six element pages or a stated Play exception: they share one shell, and the recent universal rounds have been pulling them together, not apart.

What we need back from Ben

1. A, B or C?

All three do what he asked. B is the recommendation because it costs nothing new in the palette and puts the coral where it does the most work. C is B if the page still feels flat once it is built.

2. Does this become the rule for all six element pages?

The alternation is worth having everywhere, not just on Play. If yes, it goes into the shared shell and every page gets the same rhythm; if no, Play is a deliberate exception and worth recording as one.
