What I would and would not do

Yes to the discovery-call block, and it answers the two-CTA question at the same time. That block already carries a decoration: a breathing 600px radial-gradient halo. A kit shape does not get added to it, it replaces it, which keeps the block to one treatment and swaps a generic radial gradient for a verbatim brand shape. Strongest of the four cases.

The two blocks then differ by ground, not by words. Ben's copy is identical in both on purpose, so the distinction has to come from somewhere else. With "tell the two apart" on, the mid-page block takes the shape on the right at .055 and the closer mirrors it left at .075. Same component, different weight.

Yes to shape-cropping the Foundation photo - it is a single portrait subject, which is exactly what the guidelines' expressive windows are for. Vector-7 centres the face best; Group 40273 and Vector-4 also work. Evidence at the foot of the page.

No to cropping the team photo. Twenty-five people across a landscape frame: every kit shape cuts through faces. It can still have a background shape behind a rectangular photo, which is what case D shows.

The testimonial is the weakest case. It is already the loudest thing on the page, a full coral card carrying the one real client quote. Shown so it can be ruled out by looking.

Budget note. The enablers band already carries a Crescendo wash. If the mid-page CTA takes one too, that is two shape moments a couple of screens apart - inside the "roughly one per viewport" rule, but it is the pair to check on the full page rather than here in isolation.

Photo check. The in-person photo is captioned "The Showing Up team". The embargo gate matches a word list against alt text and never looks at the pixels, so it cannot tell who is in a group shot. It is already published and presumably reviewed, but any change to how it is displayed is a sensible moment to confirm.
