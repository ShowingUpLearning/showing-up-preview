Our promise

Seven promises, made to every organisation we work with.

One of our modules, You Have to 8-Mile Them, teaches something we try hard to live by ourselves: know what you’re great at, know who you’re the right partner for, and be honest about where you’re not. So we chose to specialise. We work with companies whose teams work with clients and solve complex problems, because that’s the work we’re passionate about and love getting right. It’s a privilege to work with our clients. And if we’re the right fit for you, here is what we promise.

PROMISE 1

The best learning of your team’s careers

Our aim is simple, and it is ambitious: for the learning we design to be the most enjoyable, thought-provoking and useful of your team’s careers. Everything is story-led, grounded in behavioural science, and built for people who spend their working days in front of clients, and for the moments in that work that matter most.

PROMISE 2

Transparent pricing

Our founding team and partners have all been buyers of learning, and some of what we saw shocked us: dark arts around pricing, and programmes outsourced to associates paid 20% of the fee for doing 80% of the work. So we price simply and openly, and explain what goes into every fee. If our pricing is beyond your budget, just tell us and we’ll find a way to make it work.

PROMISE 3

The IP always transfers to you

Once your learning is designed, it belongs to you, included in the price. The intellectual property transfers to your organisation: run it with us, run it yourselves, adapt it and build on it for years to come. We think your investment should buy your team something lasting and truly yours, rather than renting you something that never really was.

PROMISE 4

Learning that sticks

Learning is only great if it creates positive and permanent shifts, which is why every module comes with its own Toolkit, every lesson ends with something your team can use straight away, and every programme supports your managers to coach the learning long after the training day itself. It is how retention and application stay above 90%, month after month after month.

PROMISE 5

A measurable impact

Learning should create lasting change, and it should deliver more value than it costs. So we agree what success looks like before we begin, and we measure everything we design against five key areas, from the reaction in the room through to the return on your investment. You will always know exactly what the learning delivered.

PROMISE 6

Building true partnerships

The people who design your learning are the people in the room with your team, and every programme is designed by our co-founder, Benjamin. We keep our client list deliberately small, so that every client receives our full attention and our very best thinking, from the first discovery conversation through to the final session.

PROMISE 7

For every person we train, a free scholarship

Showing Up began with humanitarian roots, and they remain at the heart of the company. For every person in your team we train, we provide a free scholarship to a young person facing barriers into the workplace. Our goal is to reach one million young people by 2035, and every client who works with us takes us one step closer.

100%

We are honoured to say that 100% of our clients have told us the investment was a wise one, that their people loved the learning, and that it elevated their confidence and capability in their roles.
