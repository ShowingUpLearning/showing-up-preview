5PLAY

Team games to have (competitive) fun and grow together

We've created immersive challenges for teams to play together. These video-based games are designed to be engaging, fun and competitive, with absolute utility: your team will learn something that has an immediate and meaningful impact on client work.

Two ways to play

There's two ways to play. The first is your team gets together, in a room or online, opens up the video, and simply presses play. Take Test Yourself Against Canadian Doctors: the team receives every instruction they need, along with the workbook, and away they go.

 

Manager-led sessions

Alternatively, a manager can run the game themselves. They're provided with a toolkit explaining exactly how to facilitate the session, and it's all very straightforward: in a team session, ideally in a room together, the manager presses play and coordinates the session.

Immediate impact, lasting unity
