360 assessments that reveal each person's strengths and their next step

Development moves fastest when you know exactly where you stand. This section provides engaging, in-depth 360 assessments across all Nine Dimensions™, completed solo or alongside a manager and peers. Your team can pinpoint their strengths, spot their next leap, and build a development plan of their own.

Try the two types of assessment

We've created two types of assessment. The first is what we call DISCOVER: an intriguing look at your values and preferences at work, the deeply human part of you that shapes how you navigate the world. The second is what we call ASSESS: these relate directly to the Career Development Map, measuring behaviours and skills to pinpoint exactly where your strengths and development areas lie.

Discover: Your Natural Curiosity Style

An intriguing look at your values and preferences at work. Take curiosity: it comes in three forms, epistemic, diversive and empathetic. This short self-reflection reveals your natural style, the deeply human part of you that shapes how you navigate the world.
