Introducing Spiral Dynamics: The Best Model for Understanding People

This introduction sets the foundation for our deeper Spiral Dynamics work. You will explore how human values evolve, why people see the world so differently, and how understanding these patterns improves communication, leadership and collaboration.

Self Reflection 1 – How Your Values Shape Your Worldview

This fascinating self-discovery is grounded in Spiral Dynamics. It explores your values and how they shape the way you see the world, think, react and grow. The insight gained deepens self-awareness and lays a strong foundation for meaningful growth and development.

Self Reflection 2 – How You See the World as a Consultant

Building on your personal Spiral Dynamics reflection, this self-discovery explores how your values shape how you show up as a consultant or advisor. You will see how your worldview influences your judgement, communication and client relationships in everyday work.

Stage 1 Beige: Survival, Instinct and Safety First

Stage 1 Beige is the instinct to survive. Thinking narrows to safety, shelter and immediate needs. We see it in newborn babies, in crisis situations and in organisations fighting to stay afloat. In consultancy, Beige moments call for calm, simplicity and stabilisation before growth is possible.

Stage 2 Purple: Belonging, Trust and Shared Identity

Stage 2 Purple centres on safety through connection. Identity is rooted in belonging, loyalty and shared tradition. We see it in tribes, family firms and heritage cultures. For consultants, Purple requires warmth and respect, honouring what already exists before introducing change.

Stage 3 Red: Power, Action and Standing Out

Stage 3 Red is the world of strength, assertion and decisive action. It thrives on momentum, visible wins and personal authority. In consultancy, Red shows up in fast-moving, high-pressure environments where power, confidence and quick results matter more than consensus.

Stage 4 Blue: Order, Structure and Doing What Is Right

Stage 4 Blue brings stability through systems, rules and shared standards. It values fairness, accountability and doing things properly. In consultancy, Blue shows up in governance, compliance, and structured organisations, where credibility is earned through clarity, logic, and consistency.

Stage 5 Orange: Ambition, Innovation and Measurable Growth

Stage 5 Orange is driven by ambition, innovation and measurable success. It values strategy, data and visible progress. In consultancy, Orange shows up in performance-focused organisations where evidence, efficiency and growth matter, and where achievement is the currency of trust.

Stage 6 Green: Equality, Care and Shared Humanity

Stage 6 Green centres on equality, compassion and shared responsibility. It values inclusion, dialogue and human impact. In consultancy, Green appears in purpose-led, stakeholder-focused work where culture, wellbeing, and long-term social value matter as much as results.

Stage 7 Yellow: Integration Through Systems Thinking

Stage 7 Yellow is rooted in systems thinking and integration. It sees how ideas, people and structures connect, turning complexity into coherent insight. In consultancy, Yellow helps you integrate perspectives across boundaries and design solutions that work within the whole system.

Stage 8 Turquoise: Global Integration and Unity

Stage 8 Turquoise sees humanity, nature and business as one interconnected system. It integrates systems thinking with global responsibility and purposeful action. In consultancy, Turquoise guides work that connects local impact to global wellbeing and long-term stewardship.
