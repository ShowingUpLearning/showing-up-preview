Built to be used, not filed

Less than 20% of training truly sticks. That is the market problem Showing Up was built against, and it is why a toolkit is not a tidy summary of the module you just watched. A toolkit holds all the models and things you met in the learning itself, so you can keep repeating and embedding it.

The lessons behind them work the same way. There is no theory for its own sake: each one tells you what matters and how to use it. The toolkit is what makes that portable. Before the pitch, on the train, the night before the big meeting: the learning is on demand, and the tools go with it.

All of it is mapped to the Nine Dimensions of the Very Best Consultants, so a toolkit always has an address. You know which capability it belongs to and what it is meant to move.

A toolkit sits under its dimension

Critical Thinking has four today: CriticalThinking, Coupling and Complexity, the Perrow Model, and a manager toolkit called Test Yourself Against Canadian Doctors. Creativity has SCAMPER Your Ideas, in aligned PDF and slide versions. Character is carried by the course The 12 Traits Clients Trust Most and the twelve scored self-reflections that go with it. Collaboration leans on team challenges, the platform feature built for energising ways to learn together.

Not every dimension has one yet, and it is better to say so than to pad the shelf. Communication has no standalone workbook identified; its on-platform modules carry the dimension instead. The library grows as the courses do, which is the honest version and the one worth publishing.

Toolkits for the person running the team

Not every toolkit is written for the individual. Manager toolkits are sessions a manager can run in a team meeting to create great learning together: something like objection handling, or building a great discovery meeting. A manager who wants to pull together a workshop on the spot has it ready in minutes.

One of these is live today. Critical Thinking's Test Yourself Against Canadian Doctors is a manager toolkit by name, designed to be run with a group rather than read alone. The rest are in production. This page will name what is ready rather than imply the whole set is.
