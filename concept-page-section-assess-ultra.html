Assess: 360 assessments to pinpoint your strengths and development areas

We've created a definition of what great consulting looks like: the traits, behaviours and skills of the very best consultants. Our engaging, in-depth 360 assessments can be completed by yourself, or alongside your manager and peers, to pinpoint your strengths, spot your next leap, and build your personal development plan.

Try the two types of assessment

We've created two different types of assessment. The first focuses on your natural style and your instinctive approach to work. The second measures you in depth against our Nine Dimensions methodology. Crucially, both give you something you can act on immediately in your development.

Open question
What actually makes it a 360
Needs Ben. Sharper now than it was: the new section puts the word 360 on the page three more times, in a card heading, a card eyebrow and a button.
  • Nothing built today collects a manager or peer rating. Every one of the sixteen instruments in the engine is a self-reflection.
  • How are manager and peer ratings invited, collected and matched back to a learner?
  • Do raters answer the same instrument, or a rewritten observer version of it?
  • Who sees whose scores: does the learner see named responses, or only an aggregate?
  • The 2026-07-23 design audit logged this as a P1: the homepage promises a 360, the assessments concept sells self-reflection only. The page name and the product need to agree before this goes near a buyer.
