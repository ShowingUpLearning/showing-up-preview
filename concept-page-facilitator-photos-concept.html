Facilitator photos - window shape and backdrop

Every shape still in play is on the toggle. Pick one, scroll the four backdrops underneath it, then switch and scroll again. The arch is not here - it is out.

Why the rounded options move the card radius too: two nested rounded rectangles only sit right together when the outer radius equals the inner radius plus the gap between them, so the corners stay concentric. The card pads its photo by 26px, which fixes the pairing at card = photo + 26. The card is 18px today against that 26px pad, so it is already under-rounded - even a square-cornered photo wants a 26px card. The circle is the exception: no corners to correspond with, so it leaves the card alone.

What was done to the photos: the three were shot in three different places, so each subject was cut out, then all three were normalised to the same head size and the same eye-line and colour matched. Matching the backdrop alone would not have been enough; it is the matched head size and eye-line that make them read as one set. The circle gets its own 1:1 render, framed slightly larger and more centred, because a circular window cuts the corners away.

Names, roles and bios are first drafts pulled from each person's own introduction document, for Ben to react to. Ben's card is the studio shot, not the peach one - the peach shot only exists at 333px anywhere on disk, well under what the card needs.

Photo 0px, card 26px. The concentric pair when the photo has no radius of its own. Crispest of the rounded options, and the smallest change to the card.

Photo 12px, card 38px. Both corners visibly rounded and running at the same rate. The card gets noticeably softer than it is today.

Photo 16px, card 42px. The softest pair, closest to the brand's organic rounded shapes. Past this the card reads as a squircle.
